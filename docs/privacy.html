Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 9, 2026 • Last Updated: November 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Hearsay ("we", "us", or "our"), an open-source desktop software application developed by mrpoundsign and community contributors, handles data on your computer.

1. 100% Local-Only & Zero-Login Architecture

Hearsay does not operate any centralized servers, cloud databases, user accounts, tracking, or telemetry services.

Hearsay is a standalone desktop application for Windows, macOS, and Linux. It requires zero account logins and zero OAuth credentials. All neural text-to-speech synthesis (TTS), stereo audio mixing, crowd filtering, and configuration storage execute locally on your personal computer.

We do not collect, log, transmit, or sell your personal data, chat history, listening habits, or usage analytics.

2. Direct Public Stream Connections

When you tune into a public channel on Twitch, YouTube Live, Kick, or TikTok Live, your computer connects directly to that platform's public read-only chat endpoints to receive live chat messages and public channel avatar images. For TikTok Live rooms, the open-source TikTok connector requests a public room WebCast signature from EulerStream using only the target channel's public room ID. No viewer login credentials are used or transmitted.

3. Optional Pronoun Lookups & Foreign Chat Translation

  • Twitch Pronouns Lookup (Alejo.io): When Smart Gender Voice Matching is enabled, Hearsay queries the public read-only api.pronouns.alejo.io endpoint using public Twitch usernames to match chatters with appropriate voices, caching results locally on your disk.
  • Optional Foreign Chat Translation: Disabled by default. When enabled in Settings, foreign-language chat sentences are sent directly from your computer to your selected translation provider (such as MyMemory, Google Cloud Translation, DeepL, LibreTranslate, or a local Ollama LLM).

4. Voice Model Downloads & Releases

When you download neural voice model packs or software releases, Hearsay connects directly to GitHub Releases (github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx and github.com/mrpoundsign/Hearsay) and verifies archive integrity using SHA-256 digests.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on GitHub: https://github.com/mrpoundsign/Hearsay/issues

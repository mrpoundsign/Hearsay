Listen to Live Stream Chat Like a Spatial Radio Deck

Hearsay is a free, open-source Windows, macOS, and Linux desktop application built for livestream viewers, co-stream fans, and low-vision or multitasking listeners.

Tune into one or more live streams across Twitch, YouTube Live, Kick, and TikTok simultaneously—with zero account logins, zero OAuth setup, and zero cloud subscriptions—and hear chat read aloud in real time using 100% local neural Text-to-Speech.

Zero-Auth Listener

Paste Any Stream URL or Channel Name — Instant Tune-In

Unlike streamer broadcast tools that require OAuth tokens and API keys, Hearsay is designed from the ground up as a zero-login viewer companion:

1-Click Platform & URL Detection

Select Twitch, YouTube, Kick, or TikTok and type a channel name—or simply paste any live stream URL directly into the Quick Tune bar.

Saved Streamers & Multi-Platform Creators

Bookmark your favorite creators (including multistreamers who broadcast on both Twitch and YouTube) for 1-click tuning whenever they go live.

Co-Stream & Tournament Lineups

Save multiple active streams along with their individual volume levels and Left/Right stereo panning positions as a reusable 1-click Lineup preset.

Spatial Audio Earcons

Hear subtle, stereo-panned harmonic chimes when a tuned channel goes live, goes offline, or when someone @mentions your username in chat.

Spatial Audio

Multi-Stream Stereo Panning & Audio Mixer

Watching a co-stream, squad stream, or esports tournament? Hearsay's per-stream audio mixer lets your ears effortlessly separate multiple chat rooms at once:

  • Constant-Power Stereo Panning (L100 – C – R100): Pan Streamer A to your Left ear (L75) and Streamer B to your Right ear (R75) with 1-click L / C / R buttons or a smooth slider.
  • Smart Channel-Switch Announcements: When listening to multiple streams, Hearsay announces the channel name only when the active speaker switches from one stream to another—never repeating the channel name on consecutive messages from the same stream.
  • Consecutive Speaker Coalescing: When the same chatter sends multiple messages in a row within your configured window (default 30s), Hearsay omits repeating their username so follow-up thoughts flow naturally.
  • Independent Output Device Selection: Route Hearsay to your headphones or speakers independently of your system default audio device, with instant 0%–200% master and per-stream gain controls and 0.7x–2.0x speech speed.
High-Velocity Chat

Smart Crowd Control, Self-Message Muting & @Mention Priority

Stay engaged in fast-moving streams without getting overwhelmed by chat bursts or emote walls:

  • Per-Stream Crowd Filter Modes: Set each stream independently to All Chatters, Subs + VIPs + Mods, or Mods + Streamer + @Mentions.
  • Priority @Mention & VIP Friend Bypass: Enter your own username(s) in Settings → Identity & Friends (supporting optional platform scoping like twitch:myname, youtube:@MyHandle). Whenever someone replies to or @mentions you—or when the broadcaster or one of your starred VIP Friends speaks—their message automatically bypasses Crowd Control and cooldowns.
  • Automatic Self-Message Muting: When you type in chat yourself, Hearsay recognizes your configured username (case-insensitively and ignoring leading @ symbols) and marks your message as [YOU] in the Live Log without reading your own words back to you.
  • Hype-Spam & Emote Deduplication: Automatically skips 1-word hype spam (LUL, GG, KEKW, W, F), collapses repeated emotes/words, and provides a 1-click Speak Emotes toggle right in the Live Log header.
Chatter Identity

Smart Gender Voice Matching & Deterministic Chatter Voices

Give every chatter in the room a distinct, recognizable neural voice without lifting a finger:

  • Automatic Per-Chatter Voice Hashing: Deterministically assigns each chatter a consistent voice from your enabled neural voice packs for the entire session—without cluttering your custom voice override list.
  • 3-Tier Smart Gender Matching: Automatically pairs chatters with feminine, masculine, or neutral voices using:
    • Twitch Pronouns API Cache: Asynchronous lookup and local disk caching of public pronouns via alejo.io (she/her, he/him, they/them).
    • Self-Identification Detection: Recognizes explicit pronoun self-identification in chat messages.
    • Offline First-Name Lexicon: Matches 360+ common given names inside usernames and display names, with a configurable fallback pool (Balanced 50/50, Prefer Feminine, Prefer Masculine, or Default Voice).
  • Custom Voice Pinning: Click the ⋯ menu on any chat message to pin a specific neural voice to a favorite chatter or streamer, toggle them as a Priority VIP, or mute them.
Accessibility First

UI Scaling, High-Contrast Mode, Global Hotkeys & Mini Player

Built for comfort whether Hearsay is docked beside a stream window or running in the background:

  • Live UI & Font Scaling: Adjust overall interface scaling (85% to 150% or Auto (OS)) and independent text scaling (100% to 175%) directly from the top header bar.
  • WCAG AAA High-Contrast Theme: Toggle 1-click High Contrast mode for pure black backgrounds, crisp borders, and high-contrast Deep Emerald controls, plus distinct per-chatter username colors and optional circular channel avatar icons.
  • Compact Mini Player Mode: Collapse the full Radio Deck into a compact 480×320 Mini Player showing transport controls, the Now Speaking banner, and the live chat feed.
  • Global Transport Hotkeys & Sub-50ms Panic Mute: Control playback from any application with customizable global shortcuts for Pause/Resume (Ctrl+Shift+P), Skip Current (Ctrl+Shift+S), Replay Last (Ctrl+Shift+R), and instant Emergency Panic Mute (Ctrl+Shift+M or Esc).
Offline Neural TTS

5 Local Neural Voice Packs — Shared Seamlessly with TownCrier

Hearsay shares its local neural model directory and translation/pronoun caches with TownCrier, so if you have both installed, voice models are downloaded only once:

  • Supertonic-66M INT8 (Default): Ultra-fast 44.1 kHz flow-matching voices with minimal CPU and RAM usage.
  • Kokoro-82M Neural & International (53 Voices): Studio-grade natural prosody across US/UK English, Spanish, French, Hindi, Italian, Japanese, Portuguese, and Mandarin (supports DirectML GPU acceleration and multi-threaded CPU).
  • Kitten v0.8 Mini & Nano + Piper LibriTTS: Lightweight distilled models and 904-speaker VITS voice packs for maximum variety in crowded chat rooms.
